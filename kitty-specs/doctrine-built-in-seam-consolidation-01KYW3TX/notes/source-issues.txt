#3105 — charter pack apply's config.yaml activations don't reach charter context/status without a compile step
labels: ['enhancement', 'doctrine', 'priority:P2', 'usability']
## Why

`spec-kitty charter pack apply <name>` writes pack activations into
`.kittify/config.yaml`, and `spec-kitty charter list` confirms the pack is
active there. But the governance surfaces an operator would check next don't
agree with that:

- `spec-kitty charter context --action implement` (and other actions) returns
  `active directives: []` — as if nothing were activated.
- `spec-kitty charter status` reports "Charter not found — run charter
  interview" — as if no charter existed at all, despite a populated
  `config.yaml` baseline.

So there is an unmentioned, undocumented gap between "activations written to
`config.yaml`" and "governance actually delivered to a running action or
reported by status". Nothing in the `pack apply` output, `charter list`
output, or these two commands' own messaging says a further step (compile /
interview) is required to bridge it.
