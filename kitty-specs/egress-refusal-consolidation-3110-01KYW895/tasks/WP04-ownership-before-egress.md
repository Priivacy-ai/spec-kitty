## Objective

`spec-kitty agent decision widen` resolves the repo root from the **operator's location**, not from
the **record being sent**. Standing in consenting project A and widening a decision owned by B sends
**B's identifier to A's team under A's token**, and every gate answers truthfully about the wrong
project. This is consent *laundering*, not unconsented egress.

Establish — **from local files under the acting root only, before any URL is built** — that the
acting checkout owns the decision, and refuse otherwise.

malformed vs unreadable ledgers, the forbidden repair, and the interpreter divergence. `../plan.md` →
