# rc3 fail-loud friction burndown

The eight-mission crux of rc3: turning the [friction-bug
investigation](../../investigations/friction-bugs-processing-charter-root-cause.md)
— *"a decision point defaults, drops, or short-circuits silently instead of
failing loud"* (epic **#3410**) — into a scoped, sequenced delivery. Two clusters
(charter/doctrine DRG-reach: M2/M3/M5; processing/workflow: M1/M4/M6/M8) with M7
cross-cutting.

This is a **distil-then-retire** working collection: it retires once the eight
missions land and their durable "why" is absorbed into the doctrine/charter and
execution-lanes architecture docs.

- [Program overview](rc3-friction-burndown-overview.md) — BLUF, the fail-loud

- [Delivery approach & sequencing](rc3-friction-burndown-approach.md) — the

  behavior-change sign-offs and their migration prerequisite, and the cut/land
  plan.
